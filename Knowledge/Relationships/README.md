## Circles (Interaction Frequency)

| Circle | Who | Target Frequency |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| **Inner** | Closest family & friends | Weekly+ |
| **Regular** | Good friends, close colleagues | Bi-weekly to monthly |
| **Periodic** | Extended family, acquaintances | Monthly to quarterly |

## How Reeve Uses This

- **Morning briefings**: Flag relationships that are overdue for contact
- **Relationship nudges**: "It's been 3 weeks since you talked to [person]"
- **Context enrichment**: When a person comes up in messages, provide relationship context
- **Birthday/event reminders**: Proactive reminders for important dates
